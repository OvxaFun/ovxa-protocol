Protocol

Three moves, in order

The design is small on purpose. Fewer moving parts means fewer hiding spots for surprises.

1

Bind

A token claims a single cell and names the signal that cell will follow.

2

Settle

Each update arrives inside one Solana transaction, ordered by validators and open to all.

3

Observe

Any reader may replay a cell using the ledger alone, no permission needed.

Principles

How this page behaves

Facts need a source

A detail appears here once someone has written it down and named where it came from. Until then its slot stays empty.

Switched off until real

Addresses and outbound links stay inert while they are unstated. Nothing points anywhere on a guess.

Plain machinery

Public endpoints, no private backend, no tracking. Your browser network tab shows each request.

Roadmap

Where the work goes

Phases are ordered by dependency, not by calendar.

  1. In progress

    Foundation

  2. Planned

    Open grid

  3. Planned

    Signals

    Outside feeds attached to cells, each carrying a written trust model.

  4. Planned

    Hand-off

    Upgrade rights pass over to cell holders, leaving the founding team behind.
