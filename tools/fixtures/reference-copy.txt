What it is
Ore is a front end for launching memecoins on pump.fun, with one rule: every coin is paired with a commodity. The commodity's live price sits beside the coin so the joke is legible without reading a Telegram.
Ore does not custody funds, hold keys or run its own bonding curve. Every mint, buy and sell is a pump.fun transaction you sign in your own wallet.
The markets
There are 20 markets across four sectors. Each is quoted through the US-listed fund that tracks it. Where no fund exists, Ore quotes the closest listed company instead and marks it as a stand-in.
Funds trade 9:30 to 16:00 ET, Monday to Friday, so prices hold still outside those hours. A pair is written into the coin's metadata at mint (
pair: COFFEE
) so it travels with the coin to DexScreener, Solscan and anywhere else. One market can have many coins. A coin has exactly one market.
Launching
Name (32 characters), ticker (10), thesis (180) and an optional image.
A first buy of at least 0.01 SOL goes in the same transaction, because pump.fun only shows new coins once they've had a buy.
The coin lists on Ore the moment the mint confirms on-chain. If the tab closes first, it lists next time you open the site, or from the 
register page
Launching: about 0.02 SOL for the mint and curve setup, plus your first buy and a network fee.
Trading: pump.fun's standard fee on each trade, part of which is the creator fee.
Creator fees: 100% go to the Ore treasury. The launcher receives none. This is set on-chain at mint and can't be changed later.
Treasury
treasury page
 shows the unclaimed balance live.
Memecoins are among the highest-risk assets that exist, and most go to zero. The paired commodity is a label, not a backing: holding a coin gives you no claim on oil, gold or anything else. Anyone can launch a coin with a name similar to another, so check the contract address before you buy.
Nothing on this site is financial advice.
