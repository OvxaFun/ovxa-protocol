## Intended roles of the token

The token is meant to do three jobs and no others:

- **Claiming.** Holding it is how a group claims a cell on the grid.
- **Signalling.** Holders can flag a cell whose publisher has gone silent.
- **Governance.** After hand-off, holders decide who may upgrade the program.

It is not a share in any business, and it carries no promise of income.

## Risks

Tokens on Solana are volatile and can lose all of their value. Liquidity can be thin. Programs can contain faults even after review. Nothing here is guidance on what to buy or sell.
